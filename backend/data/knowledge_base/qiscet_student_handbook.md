# QIS College of Engineering and Technology (Autonomous)
## Official Student Handbook & Academic Guidelines

### 1. Institution Overview
- **Institution**: QIS College of Engineering and Technology (QISCET)
- **Status**: Autonomous Institution, Accredited by NAAC with 'A+' Grade, NBA Accredited, Approved by AICTE
- **Academic Regulation**: R23 Academic Scheme (Autonomous)
- **Location & Contact**: Ongole, Andhra Pradesh | Website: qiscet.edu.in | Helpdesk: ithelpdesk@qiscet.edu.in

### 2. Undergraduate & Postgraduate Programs
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)**: 4-Year Undergraduate Program, Intake: 120 seats
- **B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AIML)**: 4-Year Undergraduate Program, Intake: 60 seats
- **B.Tech Computer Science & Data Science (CSDS)**: 4-Year Undergraduate Program, Intake: 60 seats
- **B.Tech Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE)**: 4-Year Undergraduate Program, Intake: 90 seats
- **Master of Business Administration (MBA)**: 2-Year Postgraduate Program, Intake: 60 seats
- **B.Sc Data Science**: 3-Year Undergraduate Degree, Intake: 60 seats

### 3. Attendance Regulations & Policy (R23 Scheme)
- **Mandatory Attendance**: Every regular student must acquire a minimum of 75% aggregate attendance in all subjects and laboratory sessions during each semester.
- **Condonation of Shortage**: Condonation of attendance shortage between 65% and 74.9% may be granted by the College Academic Committee on genuine medical grounds upon submission of valid hospital records, medical certificate, and payment of the prescribed condonation fee within 3 days of returning to campus.
- **Detention Rule**: Students whose aggregate attendance is below 65% are strictly not eligible to appear for the semester-end theory and lab examinations and will be detained. Detained students must repeat the entire semester in the subsequent academic year.

### 4. Fee Structures & Payment Procedures
- **Annual Tuition Fees**:
  - B.Tech CSE / AIML / CSDS: Convener Quota ₹45,000/year; Management Quota ₹1,20,000/year.
  - B.Tech ECE: Convener Quota ₹45,000/year; Management Quota ₹1,10,000/year.
  - MBA: Convener Quota ₹60,000/year; Management Quota ₹2,50,000 for entire course.
  - B.Sc Data Science: Convener Quota ₹40,000/year; Management Quota ₹95,000/year.
- **Payment Deadlines**: Semester fees must be cleared by the 10th of every semester's opening month.
- **Payment Gateway**: Students can pay tuition, library, hostel, and exam fees online using credit cards, debit cards, UPI, or Net Banking via the QIS Billing Gateway on the e-CAP portal.
- **Late Fee Penalty**: A late fine of ₹500 per week is levied on delayed payments after the due date.

### 5. Examination Guidelines & Timetables
- **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)**: Consists of Mid-1 and Mid-2 examinations (descriptive tests + online objective tests + assignment evaluations).
- **Semester End Examinations (SEE)**: Conducted at the conclusion of every semester for theory courses and laboratory practicals.
- **Hall Tickets**: Issued digitally on the e-CAP portal only to students with minimum 75% attendance and no fee dues.
- **Revaluation & Recounting**: Students can apply for revaluation of answer scripts through the Examination Cell within 7 days of result declaration.

### 6. Placements & Career Development
- **Training & Placement Cell**: Conducts continuous campus recruitment training (CRT), coding bootcamps, and mock interviews.

### 7. Central Library Facilities
