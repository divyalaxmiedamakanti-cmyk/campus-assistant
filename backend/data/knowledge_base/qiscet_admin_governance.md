# QIS College of Engineering and Technology (Autonomous)
## Administration Directory, Policies & Campus Governance

### 1. Institutional Governance & Accreditations
- **College Name**: QIS College of Engineering and Technology (QISCET)
- **Status**: Autonomous Institution established under Sri Nidamanuri Educational Society.
- **Accreditations & Approvals**:
  - Accredited by NAAC with prestigious 'A+' Grade
  - NBA (National Board of Accreditation) accredited undergraduate programs
  - Approved by All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi
  - Affiliated to Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Kakinada (JNTUK)

### 4. Grievance Redressal & Student Support
- **Online Grievance Submission**: Students and faculty can lodge official grievances through the e-CAP portal under the Grievances tab or write directly to `grievance@qiscet.edu.in`.
- **Grievance Redressal Committee**: Chaired by the Principal with senior faculty members and a student ombudsman.
- **Resolution Turnaround Time**: All submitted complaints are assigned a unique tracking number and addressed within 48 business hours.

### 5. Campus Safety, Discipline & Anti-Ragging Cell
- **Anti-Ragging Zero Tolerance Policy**: Ragging in any form is strictly banned on campus, in hostels, and on transport vehicles.
- **24/7 Helpline**: `+91 90000 11111` | Email: `antiragging@qiscet.edu.in`
- **Women's Protection & Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)**: Provides confidential support and addresses complaints regarding gender safety.

### 6. Official Circulars, Admissions & Degree Verification
- **Admissions**: 70% seats allocated via State Convener Quota (EAPCET / ICET / POLYCET), 30% seats via Management/NRI Quota.
- **Transcript & Certificate Issuance**:
  - Official transcripts are processed within 3 working days upon submission of the online request form and nominal verification fee at the Examination Cell.
- **Degree Verification**: Employers and universities can verify alumni credentials by emailing `examcell@qiscet.edu.in`.
